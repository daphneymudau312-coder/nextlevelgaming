About the arena

Where gaming culture meets high-performance entertainment.

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo and created for players who want more than a normal gaming experience.

Our story

Established in Johannesburg, South Africa, Next Level was founded to bring together gaming, esports, and social interaction in one premium environment. The business combines modern gaming technology with a welcoming community atmosphere.

From casual sessions to tournament-level competition, the arena is designed to support players of different experience levels. Whether you are here to relax, compete, or enjoy a group experience, Next Level provides a space that feels exciting and professional.
